## 2026-07-19: Point-in-time rating and full-sample yield panels

- Changed the clean Census-Mergent point-in-time builder to use the issuance-level rating constructed upstream as the maximum bond-level `rating_num` within each issuance (`rating_issue_max`). Missing issuance ratings are replaced with zero before calculating the usual amount-outstanding-weighted average over all bonds in the relevant category. Unrated issuances now remain in the rating denominator instead of being excluded. This is the specification that produces the previously reported 2017 full-sample coefficients of 0.276 (aggregate), 0.243 (UTGO), 0.290 (LTGO), and 0.182 (revenue).
- Added explicitly named zero-unrated rating fields for GO plus revenue, all GO, UTGO, LTGO, and revenue bonds: `mergent_wavg_rating_*_zero_unrated`. The point-in-time R script now uses these fields in every aggregate, bond-type, policy, and border-state yield regression.
- Rebuilt the 2012 and 2017 point-in-time full and border-sample CSV files from the clean Python builder and regenerated the clean R tables.
- Replaced the former three-outcome full-sample yield panel with a two-column Panel A. Column 1 regresses the full GO-plus-revenue weighted-average yield spread on `city_go_vote` without controls; column 2 adds the complete point-in-time control set. Both columns retain state-clustered standard errors.
- Added two alternative fully controlled Panel B outputs for each year:
  - `point_in_time_yield_spread_YEAR_full_sample_panel_b_utgo_ltgo_revenue.tex` reports UTGO, LTGO, and revenue yield spreads.
  - `point_in_time_yield_spread_YEAR_full_sample_panel_b_go_revenue.tex` reports all-GO and revenue yield spreads.
- The common Panel A remains at `point_in_time_yield_spread_YEAR_full_sample.tex`. The two Panel B candidates are separate fragments so either can be paired with Panel A without changing the estimation code.
- Rebuilt the 2012 and 2017 DPC point-in-time purpose panels from the current `Data/Clean_Intermediate` Census/Mergent cross-sections. The builder now matches legacy DPC bonds by normalized state and issuer name, carries the canonical `issuer_key`, preserves decimal `seed_issuer_id` values, and writes dated `260719` panel and wide files. The clean purpose-table script now reads those files and no longer screens on the removed Census total-debt control. The resulting full-sample purpose PPML panels contain 1,845 issuers in 2012 and 2,019 in 2017, all of which belong to the corresponding current clean Census sample.
- Standardized the point-in-time treatment label to `Vote` in the debt-stock PPML, purpose-substitution, and yield-spread tables. The all-GO and UTGO-only debt-choice panels retain the requested exceptions `GO Vote` and `UTGO Only Vote`; border-state and supermajority labels remain unchanged.
