\caption{\textbf{GO bond referendums and the city share of county debt}}
\label{tab:county_debt}

\parbox{\textwidth}{This table examines whether an unlimited-tax GO bond referendum requirement is associated with the share of a county's total local-government debt outstanding that was issued by city and township governments in 2017. The dependent variable equals the year-end debt outstanding of city and township governments divided by the combined year-end debt outstanding of city, township, county, special-district, and school-district governments in the county. The sample includes counties in the analysis states with at least one city or township government and positive local-government debt. Column 1 includes only the referendum-requirement indicator. Column 2 adds county economic characteristics and state policy controls. t-statistics are reported in parentheses, and standard errors are clustered by state. *, **, and *** indicate statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels.}
