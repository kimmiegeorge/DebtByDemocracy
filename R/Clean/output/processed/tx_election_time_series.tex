  \caption{\textbf{Texas GO bond referendums, website disclosure, and media coverage over time}}
  \label{tab:tx_election_time_series} 
%\small 
 \parbox{\textwidth}{This table examines time series changes in bond-related website disclosure and bond-related media coverage around elections for a sample of 872 city bond elections in Texas. The estimation samples differ across panels: \textbf{Panel A} is restricted to cities in the website collection with usable website text in consecutive years, whereas \textbf{Panel B} is restricted to cities with at least one election for which RavenPack records at least one unique source during the prior 12 months. \textbf{Panel A} examines website disclosure and reports regressions at the city-year level. City and year fixed effects are included. \textit{Increase in Bond Text} is an indicator variable equal to one for city $c$ in year $t$ if \textit{Bond Count}$_t$ > \textit{Bond Count}$_{t-1}$. \textbf{Panel B} examines media coverage and reports regressions at the city-month level. \textit{Bond Coverage} is an indicator equal to one for city $c$ in month $m$ if there was at least one bond-related media article written about city $c$ in month $m$. City and year-month fixed effects are included. In both panels, t-statistics are reported in parentheses, and standard errors are clustered by county. *, **, and *** indicate statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels. All variables are defined in \textit{Appendix A}.}
