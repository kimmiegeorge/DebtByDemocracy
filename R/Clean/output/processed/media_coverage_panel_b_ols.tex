\caption{\textbf{GO bond referendums and bond-related media coverage: Log-transformed OLS specification}}
\label{tab:media_coverage_ols_log}

\parbox{\textwidth}{This table tests whether a GO bond referendum requirement is associated with media coverage of an issuance. \textit{Log(1 + Total Articles - 12mo)} is the natural logarithm of one plus a city's number of bond-related articles from month $-12$ through the issuance month (month zero). The article count is not winsorized before the log transformation. The table reports specifications estimated using OLS. Columns 1-2 compare cities in states with a GO bond referendum requirement (green states in Figure \ref{fig:map}) to cities in states with no GO bond referendum requirement (white states in Figure \ref{fig:map}). Columns 3-4 compare cities located along the border of states with and without GO bond referendum requirements and include state-border fixed effects. All columns include fixed effects for the issuance month and Mergent project purpose. t-statistics are reported in parentheses, and standard errors are clustered at the state level in columns 1-2 and state-year level in columns 3-4. *, **, and *** indicate statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels. All variables are defined in \textit{Appendix A}.}
