\caption{\textbf{GO bond referendums and bond-related media coverage: Log-transformed OLS specification}}
\label{tab:media_coverage_ols_log}

\parbox{\textwidth}{This table tests whether an unlimited-tax GO bond referendum requirement is associated with media coverage of an issuance using an OLS specification. \textit{Log(1 + Total Articles - 12mo)} is the natural logarithm of one plus a city's number of bond-related articles from month $-12$ through the issuance month (month zero). The article count is winsorized at the 1st and 99th percentiles before the log transformation. Columns 1--2 compare cities in states with a UTGO bond referendum requirement to cities in states with no GO bond referendum requirement. Columns 3--4 limit the analysis to cities located along the border of states with and without GO bond referendum requirements. All columns include fixed effects for the issuance month and project purpose. Columns 3--4 also include state-border fixed effects. t-statistics are reported in parentheses. Standard errors are clustered by state in columns 1--2 and by state-year in columns 3--4. *, **, and *** indicate statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels. All variables are defined in \textit{Appendix A}.}
