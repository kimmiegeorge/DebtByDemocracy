\caption{\textbf{Debt Choice: Alternative Comparison States}}
\label{tab:point_in_time_debt_choice_alternative_controls}
\parbox{\textwidth}{This table reports 2017 point-in-time cross-sectional debt-choice estimates using Arizona, Colorado, Missouri, South Dakota, and Vermont as the comparison states. Panel A compares these states with states requiring voter approval for all municipal GO debt. Panel B compares them with Michigan, Ohio, and Washington, where voter approval is required only for UTGO debt. The outcomes are the shares of outstanding GO plus revenue debt represented by UTGO, LTGO, and revenue bonds. The sample is restricted to issuers with at least two GO or revenue CUSIPs outstanding. Column 1 includes only the vote-requirement indicator. Columns 2--4 add county GDP, personal income, city population, debt issued by other entities in the county, and state policy controls. In Panel B, the LTGO-allowed control is perfectly collinear with the UTGO-only vote indicator and is omitted. t-statistics are reported in parentheses, and standard errors are clustered by state. *, **, and *** indicate statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels.}
