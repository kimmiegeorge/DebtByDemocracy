\caption{\textbf{GO bond referendums and bond-related media coverage from DPC Data}}
\label{tab:dpc_media}

\parbox{\textwidth}{This table tests whether an unlimited-tax GO bond referendum requirement is associated with media coverage of an issuance using article data from DPC Data. \textit{Total Articles (DPC) - 12mo} is a city's number of bond-related articles in the 12 months leading up to the issuance. The article count is winsorized at the 1st and 99th percentiles. The sample is restricted to issuers that appear in DPC Data and have nonzero lifetime coverage, and all panels use the common complete-case regression sample. Panel A reports descriptive statistics. Panel B reports mean article counts by referendum requirement and their difference. Panel C reports fixed-effects Poisson regressions; column 2 adds county economic controls. Both columns control for the amount issued and whether the issuer sold another bond in the prior 12 months. Issuance-month and project-purpose fixed effects are included. z-statistics are reported in parentheses, and standard errors are clustered by state. *, **, and *** indicate statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels.}
